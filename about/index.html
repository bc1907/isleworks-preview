About

A small team that builds your software and stays responsible for it.

Isleworks is a software studio in Cyprus run by its two founders. The work on this desktop is real: a boutique store, a marketplace and a store assistant that are live, and a finance workspace a company's team works in. The one concept, Project Talos, is labelled as one.

You talk directly to the people writing the code. There is no account manager between your question and the person who can answer it.

How we think

  1. 01

    Show, then tell

    A working draft answers more questions than a slide about one.

  2. 02

    Built around your process

    We start from how your team works today and shape the software to fit it, not the other way round.

  3. 03

    Reliable where it counts

    Proven tools, backups we have actually restored, updates that can be undone. Novelty only where it helps your customers.

  4. 04

    Built to be maintained

    Clear code, tests that run, decisions written down. Software lives much longer than its launch week.

  5. 05

    Honest status

    If something is a prototype, we say so. This website follows the same rule.

What we do

  • Online stores

    A store you own, built around how you sell: catalogue, sizes and stock, customer accounts, email, and a back office your staff can actually use.

  • Marketplaces & platforms

    Products with many sellers or many kinds of user, with clear rules for who can see and change what.

  • AI assistants for operations

    Assistants that prepare routine work — listings, updates, replies — and wait for a person to approve it before anything changes.

  • Internal tools

    Software for a process your team runs today on spreadsheets and email: it keeps the records, checks the rules and shows who did what.

  • Running it after launch

    Hosting, tested backups, updates you can undo, and the same people on hand when something needs to change.

  • A second opinion

    A look at an existing system: what is risky, what to fix first — and, if you want, doing the fixing.
