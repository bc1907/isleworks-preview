01Store assistant · in development

Send a photo. Approve the listing. It's in your shop.

Oddkin Operator is a chat assistant for shop owners. Send it a product photo and a short note: it drafts the listing, asks about anything it can't work out, and changes nothing in your store until you approve.

Demoyu deneyin

Bu proje sayfasının ayrıntılı metni ve demosu şimdilik İngilizce. Türkçe çevirisi hazırlanıyor.

  3. 03You decideApprove, change something, or leave it

What you can ask it to do

The jobs it handles in the current development version.

  1. 01

    Add a product from a photo

    It reads the photo and your note, fills in what it can, and asks a short question about what is missing.

  2. 02

    File it in the right place

    It offers your store's own categories to choose from, rather than inventing new ones.

  3. 03

    Update prices and stock

    It prepares the change and shows exactly what will be different before anything happens.

Where you stay in control

An assistant is only useful if you can trust what it does to your shop.

  1. 01

    Nothing goes live on its own

    Every change customers would see waits for your tap.

  2. 02

    Edit after approving, and it asks again

    An approval covers exactly what you saw. If anything changes afterwards, it needs a fresh one.

  3. 03

    It only does the jobs it is given

    It works through a fixed list of store actions for your shop, and cannot reach anything else.

  4. 04

    AI spending has a limit

    Each shop has a budget, checked before every request to the AI model.

Şu anki durum

In development. Being tested against Fashion Paradise's catalogue; not yet offered as a product.
