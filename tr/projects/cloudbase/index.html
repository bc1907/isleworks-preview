04Internal finance software · in use

Booking reports in. Checked invoices out.

CloudBase is the finance workspace we built for a company in the travel sector. The team uploads booking reports, prepares and approves invoices, and tracks balances across several company entities and currencies — in one place, with a name on every step.

What the finance team does with it

The parts of the job it takes care of.

  1. 01

    Upload booking reports

    Reports from the booking system go straight in — with a warning if the same report was uploaded before.

  2. 02

    Invoice only what's open

    Rows already on an invoice are marked and can be filtered out; an invoice number can't be used twice.

  3. 03

    Approve with a name attached

    Every invoice shows who uploaded it and who approved it.

  4. 04

    Work per company entity

    Separate sales and accounting workspaces for each entity, with proformas in the format each one needs.

  5. 05

    Exchange rates that arrive by themselves

    Daily rates are imported automatically from central-bank sources and archived, so past conversions can be checked.

  6. 06

    Balances in one view

    Balance accounts can be edited, activated or filtered by status — including removing one together with its imports.

Looked after, not just launched

Internal software doesn't get a launch day. It gets every day.

  1. 01

    Updated as the team asks

    New features have been released regularly since it went live in September 2026.

  2. 02

    Updates don't sign people out

    Sessions survive a release, so nobody loses their work mid-morning.

  3. 03

    Backups that have been restored

    Backups run nightly and are regularly tested by restoring them into a scratch database.

Şu anki durum

In use on the company's own server since September 2026, and updated as the team's process changes.
