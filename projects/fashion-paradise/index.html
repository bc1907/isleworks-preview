01 — Boutique online store · North Cyprus

Fashion ParadiseYour boutique's character, on your customer's phone.

An online store built around one boutique: its collections first, quick to shop on a phone, and run day to day by the owner.

Visit the live sitefashionparadisecy.com

Discover

It opens like a lookbook.

Campaigns and collections greet visitors first, so the store feels like the boutique itself rather than a list of products. The owner changes them from the back office.

fashionparadisecy.com

Choose

Finding the right size is the easy part.

Filters for size and colour, a size chart on every product, favourites saved for later, and search across the catalogue — laid out for a thumb as much as a mouse.

Behind the counter

The owner runs the store.

New arrivals, prices and stock for each size, the homepage campaigns and the collections are all managed from the boutique's own back office. Store emails go out in the boutique's name, and replies reach the owner directly.

New products can also arrive as drafts from Oddkin Operator, a chat assistant we built for the store.
