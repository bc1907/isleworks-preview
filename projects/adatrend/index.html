Coming together

Each boutique keeps its own character.

Every shop has its own page, name and style. Together they become one catalogue, where customers browse them side by side.

adatrend.tech

One marketplace, three sides

Built for everyone who meets in it.

A marketplace only works when shoppers, sellers and the people running it each get what they need. We designed the three together.

  1. 01Shoppers

    Browse every boutique at once.

    One catalogue across all the shops, each product showing where it comes from. Guest checkout, a delivery address filled from the phone's location, and Turkish or English a tap apart.

    adatrend.tech/products

  2. 02Boutiques

    Each shop runs its own corner.

    Every boutique has its own page, and a dashboard for its products, sizes and stock, its orders, and its profile and location on the map.

    adatrend.tech/boutiques

  3. 03The platform team

    The operator keeps it in order.

    New boutiques are approved before they appear. The team can assign or hand over who runs a shop, step into any shop's dashboard to help, and look after the shared categories.

Bringing independent sellers together?

We design the storefront, the sellers' tools and the operator's controls as one marketplace.
