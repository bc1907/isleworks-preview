How a boutique gets its shop

Two ways in, so a boutique can start the way that suits it.

  1. 01

    Open it yourself

    A boutique owner creates an account and opens a shop from the site — no email to an administrator.

  2. 02

    Or have it prepared

    The AdaTrend team can set up a shop in advance and hand it to the owner with an invitation. Until it is approved, it stays out of view.

  3. 03

    Run it from one dashboard

    Products with sizes and stock, incoming orders, and the shop's profile: contact details, Instagram, opening hours and location.

What shoppers get

  1. 01

    One search across every boutique

    Search by product, boutique or category, or browse Women and Men.

  2. 02

    Honest sizes

    Sold-out sizes stay visible but can't be picked, so nobody orders what isn't there.

  3. 03

    Checkout without an account

    Guests can order; addresses can be filled in from the phone's location with one tap.

  4. 04

    Turkish first, English on request

    The whole shop switches language with one toggle.

Where it stands

Live at adatrend.tech. Early stage: the marketplace works end to end, and boutiques are being added.

In place today

  • Storefront, boutique pages and self-service shop opening
  • Boutique dashboard and marketplace administration
  • Guest checkout and customer accounts
  • Daily database backups

Not part of it yet

  • Number of boutiques, shoppers or orders
  • Revenue or growth figures
For the technically curious
Built with
Next.js, NestJS, Prisma and PostgreSQL, in Docker
Who can do what
Four roles — platform admin, admin, boutique owner, customer — each with only the access it needs
Privacy
Public pages never reveal who owns a boutique, and hidden products simply don't exist to the public
Updates
Database changes are rehearsed on a copy first, with a backup and a ready rollback
Backups
Daily, kept for 14 days

Bringing many sellers or locations under one roof?

A marketplace needs clear rules about who can see and change what. We design those together with the storefront.
