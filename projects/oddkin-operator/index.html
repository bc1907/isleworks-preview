Talk to your store. Keep the day moving.

Oddkin Operator lets a shop owner handle the store's everyday changes from a chat on their phone. Nothing changes in the shop until they approve it.

Who decides

The owner stays in charge.

  • Prices, sizes and stock come from you.

    It suggests a description and picks a category from your own list. It never guesses the numbers.

  • Every change waits for your tap.

    Publishing, prices and stock each arrive as a card with buttons. Typing “yes” is not an approval.

  • Taking a product down can be undone.

    Removing a product archives it instead of deleting it, so it can come back.
