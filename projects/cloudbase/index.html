03Finance workspace · travel & aviation

The report arrived. What has been paid, and what is still open?

CloudBase is where a travel and aviation finance team follows each report through to payments, balances and invoices — in one workspace instead of a folder of spreadsheets.

One report, followed through

From the report to the last open amount.

  1. 01

    The report comes in.

    The summary report is imported and checked row by row. If the same file is uploaded twice, the team is told before anything is counted again.
