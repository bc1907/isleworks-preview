About

We help your business go digital — and stay with you after launch.

Isleworks is a software studio in Cyprus. We build online stores, marketplaces and AI assistants, look after social media, and help businesses move the way they work onto simple digital tools.

You talk directly to the people doing the work, from the first conversation to the day it runs on its own.

What we do

  1. 01

    Online stores

    A store you own, built around how you sell: catalogue, sizes and stock, payments, email and a back office your team can use.

  2. 02

    Marketplaces & platforms

    Many sellers or kinds of user in one place, with clear rules for who can see and change what.

  3. 03

    AI assistants

    Assistants that prepare routine work — listings, updates, replies — and wait for a person to approve it.

  4. 04

    Social media management

    We plan, prepare and follow up your social media: posts, product content, messages and results, in step with your store.

  5. 05

    Going digital

    From paper, spreadsheets and phone calls to simple digital tools — set up step by step, with the training to use them.

  6. 06

    Running it after launch

    Hosting, tested backups, careful updates and the same people on hand when something needs to change.
