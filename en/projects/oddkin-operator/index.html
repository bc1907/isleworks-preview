Who decides

The owner stays in charge.

It suggests a description and picks a category from your own list. It never guesses the numbers — prices, sizes and stock come from you.

  • Every change waits for your tap. Typing “yes” is not an approval.
  • Publishing, prices and stock each arrive as a card with buttons.
  • Taking a product down archives it, so it can come back.

In the chat

What the day asks for.

It works in Telegram and is connected store by store. Its first store is Fashion Paradise.

  • Add a product from a photo
  • Find a product
  • Change a price, size by size
  • Adjust stock
  • Add a category
  • Take a product down

The product photo comes from the live Fashion Paradise store; the conversation and the store card are an illustration.
